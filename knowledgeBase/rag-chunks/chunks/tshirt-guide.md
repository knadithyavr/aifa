# The Complete T-Shirt Guide for Men

Everything you need to know about necklines, fits, fabric weights, and when to wear each type of tee.

---

## T-Shirt Necklines: What Works When

### Crew Neck -- The Universal Default

The round neckline that sits at the base of the neck with a ribbed collar. Originated from 1930s US Navy undershirts. This is the most versatile neckline in menswear.

**When to wear it:** Literally anywhere casual. Weekend outings, running errands, under a jacket, at the movies.

**Who it suits:** Almost everyone. Particularly good for men with oval and narrow faces (the round neckline adds width). Men with round faces may find it less flattering -- the circle echoes their face shape.

**The golden rule:** The collar should sit flat against your collarbone. If it sags, the tee has lost its shape. If it rides up or feels tight around the neck, it is too small.

### V-Neck -- The Face Slimmer

A neckline that dips into a V at the centre front. The V creates a vertical line that elongates the neck and visually slims the face.

**When to wear it:** Same occasions as a crew neck, but when you want a slightly more put-together look. V-necks look a touch more intentional than crew necks.

**Who it suits:** Particularly flattering for rounder and wider faces. The V narrows the visual frame. Also good for heavier builds -- the vertical line slims the torso.

**Depth matters:** A shallow V (ending 2-3 inches below the collarbone) is universally safe. A medium V is slightly more casual-stylish. A deep V that shows chest hair is a specific look -- not recommended as a starting point.

**Tip:** V-necks make excellent undershirts because the V hides beneath an open shirt collar.

### Henley -- The Casual Upgrade

A crew neckline with a buttoned placket (usually 3-5 buttons) extending down the chest. Named after the rowing town of Henley-on-Thames in England.

**When to wear it:** When you want to look like you tried a little harder than a basic tee but without putting on a collar. Perfect for casual dinners, dates, and weekend outings. Pair with dark jeans and boots for a rugged-casual look.

**Who it suits:** Everyone. The vertical placket flatters all face shapes. The buttons add visual interest to the chest area. Athletic builds look particularly good in henleys.

**Styling tip:** Full-sleeve henleys in waffle-knit or thermal fabric are excellent layering pieces. Wear them under a denim or leather jacket. Short-sleeve henleys work in summer as a standalone piece.

### Polo Collar -- The Smart-Casual Bridge

A fold-over collar with a buttoned placket. Combines t-shirt comfort with collared-shirt formality.

**When to wear it:** Office casual, client lunches, brunch, family gatherings. It is the easiest upgrade from a tee when you need to look slightly more put-together. Polos with chinos and loafers is the Indian smart-casual uniform.

**Who it suits:** Everyone, but especially beneficial for men who need to look polished without formal clothes.

**Fabric matters:** Pique cotton (the classic waffle texture) is the standard. It breathes well in Indian heat and holds its shape. Jersey cotton polos are softer and more casual. Avoid shiny, synthetic polos.

### Other Necklines Worth Knowing

- **Mock neck:** A short turtleneck (1-2 inches above the base). Sleek and modern. Good for layering under a jacket in winter or AC-heavy offices.
- **Boat neck / Breton:** Horizontal neckline that runs from shoulder to shoulder. Widens narrow shoulders. The classic French Breton stripe tee uses this neckline.
- **Scoop neck:** A wider, deeper U-shape. More relaxed than a crew neck. Summer casual.

## T-Shirt Fits Explained

### Slim Fit
Close to the body with narrower sleeves and minimal excess. Best for lean to average builds. Creates a sharp silhouette.

### Regular Fit
The middle ground. Close to the body without clinging. Universally flattering. The safest starting point for any man.

### Athletic Fit
Room in the chest and shoulders, tapered at the waist. Designed for men who lift -- bigger chest but do not want a tent at the waist.

### Relaxed Fit
Extra room everywhere without being oversized. Comfortable and breathable. Good for Indian summers when you want airflow between fabric and skin.

### Oversized Fit
Deliberately bigger -- dropped shoulders, extended length. A streetwear-influenced look popular on college campuses. Best styled with slim bottoms (skinny jeans or tapered joggers) for contrast.

## Fabric Weight: GSM Explained

GSM (grams per square meter) measures how thick and heavy a t-shirt is. This number is usually listed on the product page of online stores.

| GSM Range | Feel | Best For |
|-----------|------|----------|
| 120-150 (lightweight) | Thin, airy, drapes easily | Indian summers, layering, athletic wear |
| 150-180 (midweight) | Balanced, everyday comfort | Year-round daily wear, the sweet spot |
| 180-200 (heavyweight) | Substantial, structured, premium | Cooler months, streetwear, standalone tees |
| 220+ (ultra-heavyweight) | Thick, rigid, luxe | Winter, premium streetwear brands |

**For Indian climate:** 150-180 GSM is ideal for most of the year. In peak summer (March-June), go for 120-150 GSM. Save 200+ GSM for Delhi/North Indian winters or air-conditioned environments.

**Opacity tip:** If you do not want your tee to be see-through, avoid anything below 150 GSM in light colours. White tees especially need 160+ GSM to be opaque.

## Fabric Types

- **100% cotton (combed/ringspun):** Breathable, soft, absorbs sweat. The Indian summer champion. Gets softer with washes.
- **Cotton-polyester blend (CVC):** More durable, wrinkle-resistant, holds shape better. Slightly less breathable. Good for travel.
- **Cotton-lycra/elastane blend:** Adds stretch. Holds fit better over time. Good for slim-fit tees.
- **Tri-blend (cotton-polyester-rayon):** Ultra-soft, lightweight, excellent drape. Premium feel.
- **Bamboo or modal blends:** Extremely soft, moisture-wicking, naturally odour-resistant. Great for Indian humidity.

## Building a T-Shirt Wardrobe

### Essential Tees Every Indian Man Needs

1. **White crew neck** (2-3 pieces, 160+ GSM for opacity) -- Goes with everything
2. **Black crew neck** (1-2 pieces) -- Versatile and slimming
3. **Navy crew neck** (1-2 pieces) -- The most flattering colour for Indian skin
4. **Grey crew neck** (1 piece, heather or charcoal) -- Neutral workhorse
5. **One V-neck** in a flattering colour (burgundy, olive, or teal based on your skin tone)
6. **One henley** in a neutral colour (navy, charcoal, or olive) for casual dates and dinners
7. **Two polo shirts** (navy and white or grey) for smart-casual situations

### Brands Worth Trying in India
- **Budget:** Bewakoof, The Souled Store, HRX (decent basics at Rs 300-600)
- **Mid-range:** Uniqlo, Muji, The Bear House, Bonkers Corner (Rs 600-1200, better fabric and fit)
- **Premium:** Supima cotton range at Uniqlo, Selected Homme, Celio (Rs 1200-2500, noticeably better feel)

## When to Wear What

| Situation | Best T-Shirt |
|-----------|-------------|
| Casual weekend, mall, movies | Crew neck (any colour) |
| Casual date (coffee, walk) | Henley or solid V-neck in a flattering colour |
| Smart-casual brunch/dinner | Polo shirt |
| Under a blazer or jacket | White or grey crew neck, or a plain V-neck |
| Gym or active wear | Lightweight moisture-wicking tee |
| WFH video call | Polo or henley (looks put-together on camera) |
| College campus | Any -- crew, graphic tee, oversized, henley |
